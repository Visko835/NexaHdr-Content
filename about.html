Who is NexaHdr?

NexaHdr is a digital artifacts repository and gaming channel focused on curated save files, mods, and experimental gameplay data. Every artifact is tested, documented, and shared with a companion video walkthrough.

What started as a personal archive has grown into a community-driven collection of gameplay milestones.
